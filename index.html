Privacy Policy for Neon Snake

Effective date: 17 October 2026

1. Introduction

Neon Snake is a casual arcade game that allows players to control a snake, collect food, and earn points. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles information when you use it.

2. Information We Collect

Neon Snake does not require an account, registration, or personal information to play.

The app is designed not to collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information. It does not require access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, or photos.

3. Data Stored on Your Device

Neon Snake may store your highest score and basic gameplay preferences, such as sound settings, locally on your device.

This information supports the game's functionality and is not transmitted to us by the app. You can remove locally stored information by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app, subject to your device's behavior.

4. Internet Access and Third-Party Services

Neon Snake is designed to run offline and does not require an internet connection for its core gameplay.

The app does not include advertising SDKs, analytics services, or third-party tracking services, provided the published version remains consistent with this description.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information collected through Neon Snake because the app is not designed to collect personal information.

6. Children's Privacy

Neon Snake is a casual arcade game intended for a general audience. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

If the app is distributed to children or through Google Play's Families program, its design, content, target audience, and distribution must also comply with the applicable Google Play requirements.

7. Data Security

Gameplay information stored locally remains on your device under the protection and security controls provided by your operating system.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the app's features or data practices change. Any revised policy will be made available at the privacy policy URL associated with the app.
